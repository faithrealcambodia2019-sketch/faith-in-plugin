# Blessing UI clean update - v5.5.194

Changed only the visible Blessing story labels requested in the screenshots.

What was removed from the UI:
- The small "Text / Photo" helper row under the Add Blessing story card.
- The small "Blessing" pill label on blessing story cards and in the blessing story viewer.

What was preserved:
- Add Blessing opens the same composer.
- Blessing text posts still publish as Blessing posts.
- Photo upload still works through the Blessing composer.
- Existing feed posts, reactions, comments, stories, notifications, and other plugin functions were not removed.
