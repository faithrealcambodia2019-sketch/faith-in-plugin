# Blessing Free Music Library - v5.5.203

Added 10 built-in original 30-second Christian/worship-style instrumental tracks for the Blessing composer.

## What changed
- Users can choose one of 10 free built-in 30-second worship instrumentals.
- Users can still upload their own permitted Christian music/audio.
- Selected built-in music is saved as a safe preset media item in the Blessing post.
- Blessing viewer and feed audio player reuse the existing music player UI.
- Music-only Blessings are allowed, but users can still combine music with text and/or photo.

## Copyright safety
The bundled tracks are original short instrumental loops created for this plugin package. They are not copied from famous Christian songs or hymns and include no lyrics.
